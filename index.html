The song  /  February 2024

Four lines

One morning in February 2024, Saurabh woke up with four lines in his head. They became the opening verse of Ghosts.

It's hard to say exactly what the song is about. It's about whatever makes you think of home. Of a place. Of a person. Of a moment that never quite left you. Or one you strangely miss, even though you never lived it.

If something does that for you, it fits.

You don't need to film anything new. Go through your camera roll. Find the clip you keep coming back to: a birthday, a drive home, an empty house, a goodbye at the airport, a sky you can't explain.

Hear it

Ghosts and its video come out on 14 November. We'll email you if your moment makes the final cut.

Good to know

  • Vertical, horizontal, old, low-res: it all works. Horizontal is preferred if you have it.
  • Send the original from your gallery, not a WhatsApp or Instagram re-download, if you can
  • The sound will be replaced by Ghosts, so background noise doesn't matter
  • Videos of loved ones, including people who are no longer with us, are welcome if you're family or close to them

Please don't send

  • Videos you downloaded from the internet or someone else's account
  • Moments the people in them wouldn't want shared
  • Anything intimate, violent or private to someone else
  • Clips with text, stickers or filters added, if you have the clean version
